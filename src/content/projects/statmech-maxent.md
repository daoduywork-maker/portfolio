## The idea

Among all probability distributions consistent with what you know, pick the one with the largest entropy

$$
S = -k_B \sum_i p_i \ln p_i .
$$

Fixing only the normalisation gives the microcanonical ensemble. Fixing the mean energy as well gives the canonical ensemble, $p_i = e^{-\beta E_i}/Z$.
